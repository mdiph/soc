This is the first month, so there's no previous schedule to continue from. Tell the tool what each engineer worked the day before this month starts and how many days straight they've been on it. That's the only way it can apply the rest rules to the first few days — e.g. someone finishing their third night in a row must get an Off on day 1, not another night or a morning.

Don't know? Leave everyone on Off — the first 3 days just won't be checked against history, which is usually fine.
